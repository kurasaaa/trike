💸 Fare transparency

The big apps tell you how to get there. PANAW tells a tourist how much the tricycle should cost and why, before the trip starts.

🏛️ Ordinance-based

Fares come from the municipal ordinance and the association's fare matrix, not from estimates or crowd reports.

📶 Offline by design

The fare engine runs on the phone. Tourists with no signal in remote barangays can still check the right fare.

🤝 Built with local groups

TODAs and tourism offices supply the data. This is local knowledge that Google Maps and Waze do not collect.

Be honest in the paper: PANAW does not replace navigation apps. It fills the gap they leave, which is local fares, terminals and policy.
