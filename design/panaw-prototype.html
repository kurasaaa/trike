💸 Fare transparency
Navigation apps show the way. PANAW shows what the tricycle should cost and how that fare was computed.
🏛️ Ordinance-based
Each fare names its source: the municipal ordinance or the association's matrix.
📶 Offline by design
The fare engine and route network run on the phone, with no signal needed.
🤝 Local partners
TODAs and tourism offices supply the data. PANAW does not replace navigation apps. It fills the local gap they leave.
Location access
PANAW uses your location once to find the closest pick-up point. Nothing is uploaded, and fares work without location.
